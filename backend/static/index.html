Why are the predicted scores so low?

Expected goals is an average, not a score. A team expected to score 1.8 is most likely to score exactly 1 (about 30%), but has around a 53% chance of scoring 2 or more. Put two teams together and 1-1, 1-0 or 2-1 nearly always comes out as the single most likely score, even though no exact score is very likely.

  • Use the win / draw / loss percentages to decide who wins.
  • Use expected goals for total goals: 1.8 + 1.4 = 3.2 points to over 2.5 goals.
  • The model also bumps up 0-0 and 1-1 slightly, because real matches end that way more often than basic maths predicts.

The bookmaker line comes from the average odds across bookmakers, with their margin taken out. Odds include injuries, lineups and team news; this model only knows past results.
